An OpenCode client for iPhone and Android

Your OpenCode,
from your phone.

Connect your iPhone or Android phone to the OpenCode server running on your own PC or Mac. Away from your desk, you can still send instructions, follow progress and approve what the agent wants to do.

See how to set it up →
  • Free to use
  • English and Japanese
  • Your data stays with your server

01 / Features

Step away. The work keeps moving.

More than watching the chat: answer permission requests and questions, and check the changes, all from your phone.

Answer requests on the spot

Approve file edits and commands, and answer the agent's questions from your phone, so the work moves on without waiting for you to get back to your desk.

Get notified when it's done

Get notified when a reply finishes, fails, waits for permission or asks a question. Notifications are decrypted on your phone, so the relay can't read project names or titles.

Review changes on the go

Check changed files, diffs and branches. Browse and search files, or open a terminal.

Rewind from any message

Long-press a message you sent and choose Rewind to here to undo the conversation and the file changes after it. Its text goes back into the input, so you can reword it and send again. Undo puts everything back.

Talk to it, hear the reply

In conversation mode, what you say is sent, and the reply is read aloud when the work is done. Long-press any reply to hear it. Listening and reading aloud use your phone's own speech features.

Switch models and agents in a tap

Pick a model from one list grouped by provider and filter it by name. You can also switch agents, attach images, or stop a running reply.

02 / Setup

Connected in three steps.

All you need is a PC or Mac running OpenCode.

  1. 1

    Start the server

    Update OpenCode, choose a password, and start the server so other devices can connect.

    export OPENCODE_SERVER_PASSWORD=your-password
opencode serve --hostname 0.0.0.0 --port 4096
  2. 2

    Choose how to reach it

    At home or at work, put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as your computer. From outside, we recommend a VPN such as Tailscale rather than exposing the server to the internet. The connection guide walks through each option.

  3. 3

    Connect in the app

    Servers on the same network show up automatically. Enter the username (opencode by default) and the password, tap Connect, and your projects and sessions appear.

To get notifications too

Add "plugins": ["opencode-mobile-push"] to ~/.config/opencode/opencode.json (or opencode.jsonc) on your computer, restart OpenCode, then tap the bell at the top right of the project list and turn notifications on. OpenCode installs the plugin from npm, and the plugin makes its own pairing key on the computer. The app reads that key by itself, so there is nothing to copy.

03 / Tablets

Two panes on a bigger screen.

On an iPad or Android tablet, projects and sessions sit on the left and the chat on the right, in portrait or landscape.

04 / Price

Every feature, free.

Free

No feature limits. A banner ad appears on the project and session lists (never in chat). Once the day's free messages are used up, watching a video ad lets you send more.

Remove ads

A one-time purchase that turns off both the banner and video ads. Restore it on other devices signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

05 / FAQ

Before you install.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later, and Android phones and tablets.

What do I need?

A PC or Mac running OpenCode. The app works with servers that have the OpenCode v2 API, so keep OpenCode up to date.

Can I connect from outside my home?

Yes. We recommend a VPN such as Tailscale that puts your computer and phone on the same network. Please don't expose the server directly to the internet. The connection guide covers Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnel and SSH forwarding.

What if it won't connect?

Tap the Connection check button at the top right of the project list. It shows the server version and response time, whether events are coming in, and the state of the notification plugin. You can copy the results as text, which helps when you contact support.

Where do my chats and code go?

Only between the app and your server. The developer never receives them. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this an official OpenCode app?

No. It is an unofficial client made by an individual with no connection to the OpenCode team. It doesn't replace OpenCode itself.

How do I report a problem?

Email the address on the support page, or use Settings → Contact us in the app.
